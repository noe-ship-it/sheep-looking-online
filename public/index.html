How to play

Your mission: build a mighty flock of sheep using Sheep Head and Sheep Butt cards. The player with the biggest flock at the end of the game wins!

Setup

54 cards are shuffled. Each player is dealt 5 cards, and you only see your own hand. The rest form the draw pile, with room for a discard pile. Play moves clockwise.

On your turn

  1. Draw: always start by drawing 1 card. If you have fewer than 3 cards, keep drawing until you have 3.
  2. Play or pass: make as many moves as you like, in any order, or do nothing at all.
  3. Baa: if you have more than 7 cards, discard face down until you have 7. Challenge cards can’t be discarded. Then say “Baa!” to end your turn.

Online timer: you get 18 seconds to choose and play cards. The clock restarts every time you play a card. If it runs out, your turn ends automatically (and if you hold more than 7 cards, random non-challenge cards are discarded for you).

Making sheep

  • Basic Sheep: a head and a butt of the same colour. Worth 1 point.
  • Half Rainbow: a rainbow head with any coloured butt, or a rainbow butt with any coloured head. Worth 1 point.
  • Full Rainbow: a sheep made with 2 rainbow cards. Worth 2 points.
  • Painted Sheep: a Paint card with any colour head and any colour butt. Worth 1 point.
  • FrankenSheep: a Franken card with any 2 heads or any 2 butts. Worth 1 point. A double-headed FrankenSheep protects all sheep in its field from Wolf cards (it has eyes in the back of its head(s)!). A double-butted one protects all sheep in its field from Wheat cards (it has no mouth to eat with!).

Bodyswapping

You can bodyswap Modified sheep (Paint or Franken) or Half Rainbow sheep in any field, including your own. Swap in a head or butt from your hand so that a Basic Sheep or Full Rainbow Sheep is left behind. You take the replaced card, plus the Modifier card if there was one, back into your hand. For example, a FrankenSheep with a Green Head and a Yellow Head can be bodyswapped with a Green Butt or a Yellow Butt. This also removes a FrankenSheep’s protection!

Action cards (discarded once used)

  • Yoink!: blindly take 2 cards from an opponent’s hand.
  • Wheat: lure any 1 sheep, with its modifiers, from a field of your choice into your field.
  • Wolf: remove any 1 sheep, with its modifiers, from a field of your choice to the discard pile.
  • Re-Flip: after any coin flip, force a new guess and a new flip. You can play it even if the flip doesn’t directly affect you.

“Is That Sheep Looking At Me?” challenge cards

  1. Play a challenge card against an opponent of your choice. You can play only 1 per turn.
  2. Declare whether you think the sheep coin is (heads) or isn’t (butts) looking at you.
  3. The coin is flipped.
  4. If you guessed correctly, you use the card’s effect on your opponent. If you guessed incorrectly, your opponent uses it on you.
  • Lure 2 Sheep: move 2 sheep of your choice from the opponent’s field to yours, even past a Double-Butted FrankenSheep.
  • Remove 2 Sheep: send 2 sheep of your choice from the opponent’s field to the discard pile, even past a Double-Headed FrankenSheep.
  • Yoink Entire Hand: take the opponent’s entire hand into yours.
  • Halve 2 Sheep: steal half a sheep of your choice, plus its Modifier card, into your hand. The opponent takes the remaining half into their hand. Then repeat with another sheep.
  • Recover 1 Sheep: go through the discard pile, pick out a valid sheep (with its Modifier card if it uses one) and add it to your field.

Any challenge cards still held at the end of the game are each worth −3 points, so make sure you play them!

End of the game

Once the player who draws the last card from the draw pile ends their turn, the Final Round begins. Every player then gets a final turn, including the player who drew the last card. Then count your points. The most points wins. In the event of a tie, the sheep coin is flipped to decide the winner.
