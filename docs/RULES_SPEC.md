# Rule specification: "Is That Sheep Looking At Me? The Card Game"

> **Update:** The official Print & Play (PnP) rulebook and cards were supplied later. Where the PnP is more specific than the video, the game follows the PnP:
> - **Deck.** 54 cards: 16 colours × head + butt, **2 rainbow heads + 2 rainbow butts**, 2 Paint, 2 Franken, 3 Wheat, 2 Wolf, 2 Yoink!, 2 Re-Flip, and 5 challenge cards.
> - **Full Rainbow.** Any sheep made with 2 rainbow cards (including a rainbow FrankenSheep) is a Full Rainbow sheep and scores 2.
> - **Bodyswapping.** You can only bodyswap **Modified** (Paint/Franken) or **Half Rainbow** sheep, using a card from your hand. The swap must leave a **Basic or Full Rainbow** sheep behind.
> - **Re-Flip.** Anyone may play a Re-Flip after any coin flip, "even if it doesn't directly affect you".
> - **Hand limit.** Hand-limit discards go **face down**.
> - **Ties.** In the event of a tie, **flip the coin** to decide the winner.
> - **Final round.** The final round starts once the player who drew the last card ends their turn. Everyone then gets a final turn, including that player.
>
> The game departs from the PnP in two places, by the owner's choice:
> - **Wheat and Wolf** may target **any field**, including your own (the PnP says an opponent's).
> - **First player.** The first player is picked at random.

Source: YouTube, *How to Play: Is That Sheep Looking At Me? The Card Game!*
(ZedaphPlays, id `0o2nsB1hVK0`, 5:53). The game is by Zedaph & DotDotDash and published by CREO Cards.

## Rule specification

**Objective.** Have the highest score when the game ends (5:36–5:50).

**Players.** 2–4. This comes from the video description and the four-field layout at 0:27.

**Components.** These counts come from the publisher's Kickstarter list, *not the video*: 16 colours × (head + butt), 1 rainbow head, 1 rainbow butt, 2 Paint, 2 Franken, 3 Wheat, 2 Wolf, 2 Yoink!, 2 Re-flip, 5 ITSLAM (one per effect, consistent with "these 5 bad boys" and the 5 listed effects), and 1 coin. That makes 52 cards.

**Setup.** Shuffle. Deal 5 to each player (private). The rest form the draw pile. Choose a first player, then play goes clockwise (0:10–0:37).

**Turn.** Draw 1. Top up to 3. Then take any number of actions in any order. End with ≤ 7 cards (4:32–5:26).

**Legal actions.**
- **Make sheep** from hand: same-colour head + butt; rainbow + any; Paint + any head + any butt; Franken + 2 heads or 2 butts.
- **Bodyswap**: replace one card of any field's sheep with a head or butt from your hand so that the result is a matching sheep (same colour or involving rainbow) with no modifier. Take the replaced card and any modifier into your hand. The sheep stays where it is.
- **Yoink!**: take 2 random cards from an opponent's hand.
- **Wheat**: move one opponent sheep to your field. Blocked if that player has a double-butt frankensheep.
- **Wolf**: discard one opponent sheep. Blocked if that player has a double-head frankensheep.
- **ITSLAM**: max 1 per turn. Target an opponent, guess, flip. The winner applies the effect to the loser. Ignores franken protection.
- **Re-flip**: after any coin flip, forces a new guess and a new flip.

**Scoring.** +1 per sheep, +2 per full rainbow sheep, −3 per ITSLAM card in hand.

**End.** When the draw pile is empty, everyone gets a final turn, then scoring.

## Inferences and choices made by the implementation (not directly stated)

1. **Coin faces.** The video shows a coin with a sheep's rear and the guess "HEADS". This app uses *Looking* (sheep face) vs *Not looking* (sheep butt), a fair 50/50 drawn on the server.
2. **Who re-guesses after a Re-flip.** "Force a new guess and a new flip." The app has the ITSLAM player make the new guess. Either party may play a Re-flip when the flip went against them, and Re-flips can chain while cards remain.
3. **Bodyswap result must be a natural match** (no modifier). This reflects every example plus "complete matching sheep". The app does not allow bodyswapping a modifier card onto an existing sheep, because the video never shows it.
4. **Wheat/Wolf targets** are opponents' sheep. The narration says "anyone's"/"someone's", but targeting yourself is pointless for Wheat and self-harming for Wolf. *Wolf on your own sheep is disabled.*
5. **Recover 1 sheep** may use a modifier from the discard pile if one is there ("play any valid sheep from the pile").
6. **Halve 2 sheep / Lure 2 / Remove 2** against a field with fewer than 2 sheep affects as many as exist.
7. **Final round.** After the turn in which the draw pile becomes empty, every player, starting with the next one, gets exactly one more turn, so the player who emptied it goes last. Drawing from an empty pile does nothing. The discard pile is never reshuffled.
8. **First player.** "Most recently saw a real sheep" can't be verified online. The app picks the first player at random.
9. **Ties** are not covered in the video. Tied top scores are declared joint winners.
10. **Colour names.** The video frames show cyan, magenta, mint, orange, green, red, yellow, purple, grey, pink, black, brown, blue, lime, beige, and more. The app uses 16 original colour names. Card artwork is original CSS, not copied.
11. **Wolf/Wheat/Yoink with no legal target** cannot be played.

## Still unclear

- The exact deck composition is not shown in the video (the Kickstarter list is used).
- Whether a Re-flip can be played by a third player who is not involved in the flip. The app says no.
- Whether a player may bodyswap using a modifier card from hand (one fan wiki suggests yes). The app does not allow it.
- Whether the active player must keep playing after the hand limit is reached. The app only enforces ≤ 7 to end the turn.
